Security Incident Desk

A practical workspace for documenting suspicious messages and preserving evidence.

Incident checklist

  • Preserve the original email or message.
  • Record when and where it was received.
  • Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
  • Verify the sender through a trusted, independent channel.
  • Redact passwords, tokens, and personal information before sharing evidence.

Privacy and limitations

This static website does not identify visitors, collect their IP addresses, or run server-side access logging. Email header results may be incomplete or forged. Treat them as clues, not proof.

Only collect or share evidence you are authorized to handle. Keep original evidence secure and restrict access to sensitive incident records.
